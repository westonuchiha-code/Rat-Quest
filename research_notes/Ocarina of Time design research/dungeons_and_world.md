## 1. World structure: Hyrule Field hub, child/adult split, optional content, Field size/emptiness/Epona

### Takeaway
Developers say Hyrule Field was built big so Link could ride a horse, and it was initially too empty, so they cut "holes" and put things in it. The detailed mechanics of region gating and optional content (heart pieces, grottos, minigames) were NOT covered by anything I could read.

### Cited Findings
- Developer statement (Iwata Asks, Miyanaga, via search summary): the vast field was meant for Link to ride a horse, and making it "created quite a fuss"; the team found it "a bit empty", so they would "make a hole and put something in it". Paraphrased, second-hand. — [Iwata Asks p2/4](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/2/4) (not fetched)
- A Zelda Dungeon translation of a developer interview says that for a long time Hyrule Field "had nothing, it was terribly uninteresting", and that wind, temperature and atmosphere effects were planned to make the plain feel alive. Second-hand summary. — [Interview: Link's Horse](https://zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Interview:Ocarina_of_Time_Development_-_Link%27s_Horse) (not fetched)
- Miyamoto interview (newly translated, per a Time Extension report) links Epona's role to Princess Mononoke, developed at the same time, and says the team built up Hyrule to differentiate it. Unverified detail. — [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2025/02/random-miyamoto-was-surprised-at-the-similarities-between-ocarina-of-time-and-princess-mononoke)
- Miyamoto: "No one knew how big in scale it would become" (2011 Iwata Asks, via secondary). — [Nintendo World Report](https://nintendoworldreport.com/news/26851) (not fetched)
- Per a search summary, the Switch 2 remake's team added warp points because players spent time rolling across Hyrule Field; the source is a gamerfuzion page I could not verify and this is a 2026 claim. UNVERIFIED. — [Gamerfuzion](https://www.gamerfuzion.com/zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-ask-the-developer-team-revealed-warp-points-cut/)
- Gold Skulltula hunt criticism: reward for all 100 is only 200 rupees (an "almost pointless" item late in the game); the hunt is "arduous". Contrast: Majora's Mask puts its Skulltulas in two Spider Houses. Some Skulltulas are only reachable as one age. Critic opinion. — [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/zelda-ocarina-time-gold-skulltula-reward-bad-stupid/), [ScreenRant MM comparison](https://screenrant.com/zelda-gold-skultulla-ocarina-time-majoras-mask/), [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/reviews/rewind-review-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-oot-3ds/)

### Inferences
- The "make a hole and put something in it" remark suggests field content (grottos, ranch, etc.) was added in response to emptiness rather than planned first. Inference only; verify with the full Iwata Asks text.
- Collectible criticism converges on a reward-effort mismatch, a useful warning for a hub-and-levels game.

## 2. Dungeon structure and case studies

### Takeaway
Only fragments obtained: Deku Tree is treated by critics as a tree-shaped, nearly linear tutorial; Forest Temple's central room is praised as the navigational anchor with visible state change; the Water Temple is described as a tower hub with three water levels.

### Cited Findings
- Inside the Deku Tree: layout echoes a tree with a tall central trunk and branch-like room sets; more linear than labyrinthine, no keys, little interconnection, which suits a tutorial. Critic analysis. — [Zelda Dungeon: Inside the Deku Tree](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=47889) (via search summary)
- Same dungeon teaches vine climbing, timer switches, moving blocks, swimming, torch puzzles (separate piece, via search summary). — [VG Thought OoT dungeon project](https://vgthought.com/project/oot_dungeons)
- Forest Temple: torches in the central room light after each Poe is defeated, so returning to the hub shows visible progress; the hub is what makes backtracking work. Critic opinion. — [Zelda Dungeon: Brilliance in Level Design, Forest Temple](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=10887)
- Water Temple: described as a central tower-like hub with three levels and a basement. — [Zelda Dungeon: Brilliance in Level Design, Water Temple](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=11084)
- Spirit Temple: described by one critic as OoT's "most versatile dungeon" (child and adult halves; some Skulltulas reachable at one age only). — [Zelda Dungeon](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=196115)
- Dodongo's Cavern and Ganon's Castle: no meaningful analysis found.
- Developer: even late in development, items had to be moved between dungeons (Osawa, Iwata Asks, paraphrased). — [Iwata Asks p1](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/1/0) (not fetched; see search summary via [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/?p=160942))
- Developer interview on dungeons (original staff): [glitterberri Dungeons Part 1](https://glitterberri.com/ocarina-of-time/1101-interviews/dungeons/) and [Zelda Dungeon Part 1](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Interview:Ocarina_of_Time_Development_-_Dungeons_Part_1) exist and are the primary sources to read; contents not obtained.

### Inferences
- Central-room-with-state-change (Forest Temple torches, Water Temple tower) supports the "coherent place" question (Q7). Inference from critic claims only.

### Gaps
- Standard structure details (map/compass/small keys/boss key/mid-dungeon item), Dodongo's Cavern, Ganon's Castle recap: not obtained.

## 5. What developers said about dungeon design and development

### Takeaway
Best-supported section: Miyamoto's early Ganon's-Castle-hub fallback, shared Mario 64 lineage, Aonuma's Water Temple regrets, Master Quest origin.

### Cited Findings
- Early plan: Miyamoto reportedly considered setting the game in/around Ganon's Castle as a Mario 64-style hub with painting-like portals, as a worst case / memory-saving fallback since he was unsure Hyrule Field was possible. Sources differ on how firm the plan was (Nintendo Life: "worst case scenario"). — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2011/06/ocarina_of_time_at_one_point_confined_within_ganons_castle), [Zelda Universe](https://zeldauniverse.net/2011/06/29/ocarina-of-time-development-details/), [Nintendo World Report](https://nintendoworldreport.com/news/26851)
- Miyamoto (via search summary): the Phantom Ganon fight at the end of the Forest Temple is a leftover of the all-in-Ganon's-Castle (paintings) idea. — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2011/06/ocarina_of_time_at_one_point_confined_within_ganons_castle) (secondary; the aggregator-level detail is lower quality)
- Aonuma (Iwata Asks, 3D): he insisted on remaking the Water Temple, "I'm the one who placed a burden on everyone to satisfy my desire to remake the Water Temple"; called it "a bone caught in your throat for 13 years". — [Iwata Asks p3/2](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/3/2), [Zelda Dungeon](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=2585), [ZeldaUniverse](https://zeldauniverse.net/2020/03/03/zeldas-study-the-regret-that-haunted-eiji-aonuma-for-13-years/)
- Aonuma 2009: "I should have made it much easier to switch to the heavy boots." — [Game Informer 2009](https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2009/11/29/aonuma-apologizes-for-water-temple.aspx), [Gamezone](https://gamezone.com/?p=59609)
- Aonuma also calls the Water Temple one of his favorites. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/legend-zelda-ocarina-time-water-temple-facts-trivia/) (secondary)
- Aonuma (Majora's Mask 3D Iwata Asks): Miyamoto asked him to build Master Quest (64DD) by repurposing existing assets; he reluctantly agreed, and secretly began making new dungeons, which led toward Majora's Mask. — [Zelda Dungeon](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=11277)
- Remake-era Aonuma quote (2026, UNVERIFIED by me): Water Temple difficulty "came from the system that we were presenting to players". — [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/interview/2026/11/03/zelda-ocarina-remake-producers-talk-changing-and-maintaining-the-game-easter)
- Mario 64 shared engine / Aonuma GDC 2004 talk / playtesting / cartridge-space cuts: not obtained. Primary lead: [Half Done By Mario interview](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Interview:Ocarina_of_Time_Development_-_Half_Done_By_Mario).

### Inferences
- Aonuma's Water Temple remarks point to a lesson about friction in required state-changing items (boots), consistent with Mark Brown's polarizing framing below.

### Gaps
- Aonuma's exact role as dungeon designer, GDC 2004, cartridge cuts, playtesting: unread.
