## Combo system, defence, and movement abilities (Questions 1-3)

### Takeaway
Only high-level ability facts were obtained: each Drive Form owns one growth ability that also upgrades for base Sora, and Reflect/Aerial Recovery are described qualitatively. No frame windows, speed numbers or combo-string details were found.

### Cited Findings
- Valor Form's growth ability is High Jump. Wisdom's is Quick Run, and levelling Wisdom upgrades Quick Run for Wisdom and base Sora. Final Form's is Glide, upgraded for itself and base Sora. Master Form pairs with Aerial Dodge. Limit Form (Final Mix only) has Dodge Roll. Limit Form can block without the spin and jumps slightly higher to compensate for lacking High Jump. (Secondary fan wiki, as summarised by search) — [KH Wiki Drive Form](https://khwiki.com/Drive_Form); [KH Wiki Growth Ability](https://www.khwiki.com/Growth_Ability); [StrategyWiki abilities](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_II/Abilities); [KH Wiki Limit Form](https://www.khwiki.com/Limit_Form)
- Final Form also grants Auto Final and two Form Boost abilities to base Sora — [KH Wiki Final Form](https://khwiki.com/Final_Form)
- Maxed growth abilities gate platforming in the Final Mix Cavern of Remembrance; a forum poster says one gap needs Quick Run (or harder, Glide) then Aerial Dodge — [TV Tropes KH2](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/KingdomHeartsII/TropesAToL); [KH-Vids thread](https://kh-vids.net/threads/abilities-needed-for-cavern-of-remembrance.148931)
- Aerial Recovery lets Sora right himself after being knocked airborne and grants brief invulnerability and the ability to parry — [KH Wiki Aerial Recovery LV3](https://khwiki.com/Aerial_Recovery_LV3)
- Aerial Dodge has more invincibility frames than one Air Slide but travels less and is slightly slower — [KH Wiki Aerial Dodge](https://www.khwiki.com/Aerial_Dodge)
- Reflect Combo: pressing Square (Guard) during a combo blocks enemy attacks; Reflect Guard blocks and returns attacks as a parry — [KH Wiki Reflect Combo](https://www.khwiki.com/Reflect_Combo)

### Inferences
- Because each form's ability is shared with base Sora, form levelling doubles as permanent movement upgrades, which is the likely mechanism behind late-game Sora being much more mobile (not confirmed by a source read here).

## Enemy and boss internals: revenge value, safety abilities, case studies (Question 5)

### Takeaway
Revenge Value is a hidden per-enemy threshold that makes a boss break a combo with a Revenge Action; magic and several finishers do not add to it, and a 20-second timer forces an action. Exact values and tables were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Each boss has a hidden threshold; Sora's hits add to a running total and passing it triggers a Revenge Action. "Revenge Value" is a fan name, official term "Revenge Action". Magic, magic combo finishers, reprisals and finish commands for Formchanges, Team Attacks, Links and Attractions add none. If any value has accumulated for 20 seconds the enemy uses a Revenge Action regardless of threshold. Thresholds are hardcoded and hidden (search-summary of wiki and community pages) — [KH Wiki Revenge Value](https://www.khwiki.com/Revenge_Value); [KH Wiki Revenge Action](https://www.khwiki.com/Revenge_Action); [Critical Points](https://critpoints.net/2018/02/19/kingdom-hearts-2-revenge-value)
- Lingering Will has a very high threshold and is vulnerable to long combos; the wiki recommends Combo Boost/Air Combo Boost and Combo/Air Combo Plus; another guide says to skip Guard Break and use Explosion when deliberately triggering its Revenge Value — [KH Wiki Lingering Will](https://www.khwiki.com/Game:Lingering_Will)

### Inferences
- The system is a fairness/anti-spam rule: unlimited combos are allowed but are punished by a readable-in-hindsight counter, which suits a Souls-like design where greed is punished (inference, not from a source).

## Critical Mode and reception (Questions 6 and 8)

### Takeaway
Critical Mode in Final Mix halves HP and doubles damage taken per fan sources, and commentators say it forces use of the full toolset. Numbers are fan-sourced and need verifying.

### Cited Findings
- A fan post says Critical gives 0.5x max HP (60 HP) and 2x damage taken versus Standard, that Roxas starts at 50 AP with +3 per level, and that it discourages Cure/Cura/Curaga abuse in favour of deflecting hits — [Minishlan, "Why KH2FM is great"](https://minishlan.github.io/whykh2fmisgreat.txt)
- A game-design blog argues that for an expert, these systems must be used or Critical Mode is insurmountable, and that players are heavily rewarded for engaging with the game's complexity — [Game Balance in Kingdom Hearts II (Medium)](https://medium.com/game-design-fundamentals/game-balance-in-kingdom-hearts-ii-417db8cee9b3)
- A Mein-MMO player piece calls Critical Mode a way to reveal the combat's potential and rates it among the best action combat systems — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/for-9-years-i-avoided-the-hardest-test-of-my-favorite-arpg-now-i-would-recommend-it-to-everyone,1590900/)
- RPGFan describes the balance as honest combat design plus post-God of War cinematic spectacle — [RPGFan Retro Encounter](https://rpgfan.com/feature/retro-encounter-final-thoughts-kingdom-hearts-ii)
- A dissenting Backloggd review calls the system feeble and easily broken by observation — [Backloggd](https://www.backloggd.com/u/rubenmg/review/789632/)

### Inferences
- Critics' praise clusters on Critical Mode, suggesting the system's depth only shows when mistakes are costly (inference).

### Gaps
- Mashing on lower difficulties, camera and floatiness criticisms: only the dissent above; no sourced detail.
- Video essays and speedrun/Level 1 Critical documentation: not found.
