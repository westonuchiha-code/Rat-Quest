## 1. Connected, vertical world layout and shortcuts

### Takeaway
Developer-side evidence found is limited to a Famitsu Design Works interview (Miyazaki and artists) reportedly stating that the world was designed as one large connected dungeon, map-first. The specific "map designed in 3D" and "shortcut opening" Miyazaki quotes were NOT found for Dark Souls.

### Cited Findings
- The Design Works interview was conducted by Famitsu's Kadoman Otsuka with Miyazaki and several artists (incl. Hiroshi Nakamura, Masanori Waragai). Mirrors exist at Fextralife, Souls Lore and Giant Bomb. — [Fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+1+-+Design+Works+Interview), [Souls Lore](https://soulslore.wikidot.com/das1-design-works), [Giant Bomb](https://www.giantbomb.com/forums/dark-souls-6255/dark-souls-design-works-translation-creating-the-w-567717/) (existence/attribution via search summary only; text not read)
- Per search summaries of that interview: Dark Souls' world was designed as one large, full dungeon, unlike Demon's Souls' separate stages, and the whole world had to look natural as players walk between areas. — [Fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+1+-+Design+Works+Interview) (paraphrase from search summary, unverified wording)
- Per the same summaries: a rough map of each area was drawn first and finer details followed once the basic layout was decided. — [Fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+1+-+Design+Works+Interview) (paraphrase, unverified wording)
- A 2011 interview reportedly said the map would be about 1.5x the size of Demon's Souls'. — [GameFAQs thread](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/606312-dark-souls/62421358) (fan forum, secondhand)
- The "three-dimensional, fully interconnected" phrasing found is attributed to a Famitsu interview about Sekiro, where Miyazaki said Sekiro's map design is much like Dark Souls 1's. This is about Sekiro, not Dark Souls. — [Medium, World Design lessons from FromSoftware](https://medium.com/@Jamesroha/world-design-lessons-from-fromsoftware-78cadc8982df) (blog, secondary)
- A fan forum post claims the Design Works book mentions a very hard-to-find shortcut bypassing Ceaseless Discharge that "border line seems like a glitch". Unverified fan account. — [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/606312-dark-souls/62421358)
- Game Maker's Toolkit (Boss Keys) episode "The World Design of Dark Souls" exists (2018-04-25); it covers how Lordran is put together, advantages/challenges of non-linear worlds, and similarity to Zelda. Transcript not read. — [Amara subtitles](https://amara.org/subtitles/4zZSGmv4Qxsz/en/1/download/The%20World%20Design%20of%20Dark%20Souls%20%20Boss%20Keys.en.txt) (search summary only)

### Inferences
- The map-first, one-dungeon framing supports borrowing "hub plus loops back to hub" structure, but the team should read the Design Works text directly before quoting it.

### Gaps
- Exact Miyazaki quotes on 3D map design and the joy of a shortcut opening: not found.
- Specific routes (Firelink, Undead Burg/Parish loop, lift, Blighttown, Sen's Fortress, Anor Londo) with sources: not researched (fetch blocked).

## 2. Bonfires, shortcuts, boss runs, pacing; Lordvessel and the second half

### Takeaway
Only community opinion found: Lost Izalith is the most-cited weak area; the "second half is weaker" claim is disputed; "rushed" is not confirmed by developer statements in what I could access.

### Cited Findings
- Forum users single out Lost Izalith ("cool setting, awful level design"; wide empty spaces, recycled enemies); Demon Ruins, Crystal Caves and Tomb of Giants are also named as weaker. OPINION. — [ResetEra thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/the-dark-souls-has-a-bad-second-half-meme-is-overblown-imo.61495/page-2), [ResetEra dev question](https://www.resetera.com/threads/what-happened-during-the-development-of-dark-souls-that-made-its-second-half-worse.93646/)
- Others say the "bad second half" is overblown, that the DLC (in the second half) has the best bosses, and that Lost Izalith is short. OPINION. — [ResetEra thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/the-dark-souls-has-a-bad-second-half-meme-is-overblown-imo.61495/page-3)
- Lost Izalith (chaos/fire/demons) was planned as one of three main thematic sections alongside Anor Londo and Gravelord Nito's area per the Design Works interview (search summary). — [Fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+1+-+Design+Works+Interview)

### Gaps
- Any Miyazaki/FromSoftware statement on cut or rushed content (Lost Izalith, Demon Ruins): not found. Widely repeated "ran out of time" claims are unverified here.
- Critic analyses (Eurogamer, Edge, Game Developer, "You Died"): not accessed.
- Effect of warping after the Lordvessel on pacing: no sourced material.
